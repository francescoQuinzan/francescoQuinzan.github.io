I am a Senior Research Associate at the University of Oxford, where I lead research on reliable machine learning and AI safety, and I am affiliated with the Centre for Human-Inspired Artificial Intelligence (CHIA) at the University of Cambridge. My research develops principled methods to understand and evaluate how foundation models evolve under feedback and adaptation. I have authored several peer-reviewed works, most of them as lead author or senior contributor, including publications at NeurIPS, ICML, AISTATS, and Transactions on Machine Learning Research.

Before taking up my current senior position at Oxford, I was a Research Associate at the University of Oxford from 2023 to 2025, where I conducted research in safe and reliable machine learning and was a member of the European Lighthouse on Secure and Safe AI (ELSA) network of excellence. Prior to Oxford, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, working in the Division of Decision and Control Systems. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Hasso Plattner Institute and previously earned an M.Sc. in Mathematics from Roma Tre University, graduating summa cum laude with the highest grade and an honourable mention.

My research has received substantial competitive support and recognition, including a UKRI AIRR Gateway Award providing access to the Isambard-AI supercomputing facility, a BlueDot Compute Grant for AI safety research, ELSA and HCHE Research Mobility Awards, and multiple COST Short-Term Scientific Missions. I was also selected for the Iliad Intensive with a competitive full bursary and for the University of Oxford Saïd Business School’s Ideas 2 Impact programme. I am a member of the Future of Life Institute’s AI Existential Safety Community and a Fellow of Advance HE.

Alongside my research, I am active in the international machine-learning community. I serve as an Area Chair for NeurIPS 2026 and am the lead organiser of the ELLIS Workshop on LLM Post-Training, sponsored by NeurIPS 2026. I have served on the programme committees of leading machine-learning and AI venues, including ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, UAI, AAAI, AISTATS, and JMLR.
